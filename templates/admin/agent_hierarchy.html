💡 TAIKO EA Override Commission Model

📊 Personal Payout

Each agent earns their rank % on every deal they personally close. REN 70% → ATL 90%.

⚡ Override Earnings

Upline earns (their % − downline %) × downline's team gross. e.g. TL 85% over REN 70% = 15% override.

🔁 WTP Bonus

Same-rank downline: override = 0, but upline earns WTP 2% (Gen1) or 1% (Gen2) instead.

🏅 Auto Promotion

Rank upgrades at RM 30k / 90k / 210k / 450k personal cumulative gross. New rate from next deal.
